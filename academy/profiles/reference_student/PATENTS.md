# Overview

Innovation is a continuous engineering activity.

This portfolio documents engineering inventions, patent candidates, intellectual property initiatives, and technology concepts developed throughout my professional journey.

Rather than focusing solely on granted patents, this portfolio demonstrates the complete innovation lifecycle—from idea generation and research through engineering validation, intellectual property protection, and potential commercialization.

It serves as a reference model for future FAEP Academy learners interested in transforming engineering ideas into valuable intellectual property.
