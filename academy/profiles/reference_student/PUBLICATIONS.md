# Publications

> *A portfolio of engineering knowledge assets including research papers, white papers, technical documentation, reference architectures, educational resources, open-source projects, and engineering frameworks.*

# Overview

Knowledge is one of the most valuable assets an engineer creates throughout a professional career.

This publication portfolio documents the evolution of engineering knowledge developed through research, software engineering, systems engineering, enterprise architecture, Artificial Intelligence, Quantum AI, education, and innovation.

Rather than limiting publications to traditional academic papers, this portfolio recognizes the many ways engineering knowledge can be shared, preserved, and reused by the wider community.

It serves as a reference model for future FAEP Academy learners to document and showcase their own professional contributions.

---

# Publication Philosophy

Engineering knowledge should be:

- Practical
- Reusable
- Well documented
- Accessible
- Continuously improved
- Openly shared whenever appropriate

Publications are viewed as living knowledge assets that evolve alongside research, engineering practice, and lifelong learning.
