# Career Roadmap

> *A lifelong journey of continuous learning, engineering excellence, research, innovation, and knowledge sharing.*

---

# Overview

Every engineering career is unique.

Rather than viewing a career as a sequence of job titles, this roadmap illustrates the continuous development of knowledge, skills, professional responsibility, and engineering capability.

It serves as a reference roadmap for FAEP Academy learners, demonstrating how curiosity, lifelong learning, and practical experience can shape a rewarding engineering career.

---

# Career Philosophy

A successful engineering career is built upon:

- Curiosity
- Continuous Learning
- Engineering Excellence
- Research
- Innovation
- Collaboration
- Professional Integrity
- Knowledge Sharing

Technology changes rapidly, but strong engineering principles remain timeless.
