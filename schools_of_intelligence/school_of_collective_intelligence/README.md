# School of Collective Intelligence

> *Engineering intelligent ecosystems where people, organizations, software agents, machines, and knowledge collaborate to solve complex problems beyond the capability of any individual system.*

---

# Overview

The **School of Collective Intelligence** explores how intelligence emerges from collaboration among individuals, autonomous agents, organizations, digital ecosystems, and distributed systems.

Rather than focusing on a single intelligent entity, this School studies how networks of intelligent participants cooperate, coordinate, share knowledge, and make collective decisions across enterprise, scientific, governmental, industrial, and global ecosystems.

This School represents one of the foundational pillars of the **Federated Autonomous Ecosystem Platform (FAEP)**.

---

# Vision

Develop engineers, architects, researchers, and innovators capable of designing scalable collective intelligence ecosystems that combine human expertise, Artificial Intelligence, autonomous machines, digital twins, enterprise platforms, and knowledge networks.
