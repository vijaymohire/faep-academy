# School of Autonomous Machines

> *Engineering intelligent machines capable of sensing, reasoning, learning, collaborating, and acting autonomously within physical and digital environments.*

---

# Overview

The **School of Autonomous Machines** focuses on the engineering of intelligent physical systems that combine Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Cyber-Physical Systems, Digital Twins, Embedded Systems, and Autonomous Decision-Making.

Learners develop the knowledge and practical skills required to design, build, validate, and operate autonomous machines across industrial, scientific, commercial, healthcare, transportation, defense, and space applications.

This School integrates software intelligence with hardware engineering to prepare professionals for the next generation of autonomous systems.

---

# Vision

Develop engineers and researchers capable of building trustworthy, safe, scalable, and adaptive autonomous machines for Industry 5.0, Society 5.0, Smart Cities, Intelligent Infrastructure, and Future Space Systems.
