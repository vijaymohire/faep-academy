# Schools of Intelligence Roadmap

> **Building the Next Generation of Professional Intelligence Development**

---

# Overview

The Schools of Intelligence Roadmap defines the long-term evolution of the FAEP Academy's professional capability development ecosystem.

The roadmap provides a phased approach for expanding educational content, practical learning experiences, professional certifications, applied research, and industry collaboration.

The objective is to create a sustainable learning ecosystem that continuously evolves alongside emerging technologies and industry needs.

---

# Vision

Develop one of the world's most comprehensive practical learning ecosystems for Human Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, Autonomous Machines, Collective Intelligence, and future intelligent systems.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The roadmap supports the following long-term objectives.

- Build practical learning paths
- Develop role-based education
- Expand hands-on laboratories
- Strengthen engineering competencies
- Foster innovation and entrepreneurship
- Support lifelong professional learning
- Promote responsible intelligence
- Build a global learning community
