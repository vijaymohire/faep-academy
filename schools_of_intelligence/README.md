# Schools of Intelligence

> **Practical Intelligence. Real-World Skills. Future Careers.**

---

## Overview

The **Schools of Intelligence** is a professional capability development framework within the **FAEP Academy**. It provides structured learning paths that prepare learners, professionals, researchers, entrepreneurs, and organizations for the next generation of intelligent systems.

Unlike traditional academic institutions that focus primarily on degrees, the Schools of Intelligence emphasize **practical skills**, **hands-on experience**, **industry readiness**, **innovation**, and **lifelong learning**.

The objective is to develop individuals capable of designing, building, deploying, and managing intelligent systems across multiple domains while fostering responsible innovation and human-centered technology.
