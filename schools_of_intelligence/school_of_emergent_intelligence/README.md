# School of Emergent Intelligence

> *Understanding and engineering intelligence that emerges from complex interactions among autonomous agents, adaptive systems, networks, and dynamic environments.*

---

# Overview

The **School of Emergent Intelligence** explores how intelligent behavior can emerge from interactions among multiple independent components without requiring centralized control.

Rather than programming intelligence directly, this School investigates the principles of self-organization, adaptation, emergence, complexity, evolutionary systems, and adaptive ecosystems.

Learners study how large-scale intelligent behavior develops naturally from local interactions across biological systems, digital ecosystems, distributed computing, autonomous agents, cyber-physical systems, and future intelligent infrastructures.

This School represents an important research frontier within the **FAEP Academy**, connecting complexity science, artificial intelligence, systems engineering, and future autonomous ecosystems.
