# School of Human Intelligence

> *Understanding, developing, and augmenting human cognitive, creative, emotional, ethical, and collaborative capabilities for the future of intelligent societies.*

---

# Overview

The **School of Human Intelligence** focuses on understanding how people perceive, learn, reason, create, communicate, make decisions, and collaborate within increasingly intelligent environments.

Rather than replacing human intelligence, this School emphasizes enhancing human capabilities through education, cognitive science, systems thinking, neuroscience, human-centered design, and responsible technology.

The School prepares learners to become effective engineers, researchers, leaders, educators, innovators, and decision-makers capable of thriving alongside Artificial Intelligence and Autonomous Systems.

---
