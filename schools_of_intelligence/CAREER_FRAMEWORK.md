# FAEP Academy Career Framework

> **Building Professional Capabilities for the Intelligence Economy**

---

# Overview

The FAEP Academy Career Framework provides a structured approach for developing professional capabilities aligned with emerging technologies, intelligent systems, engineering disciplines, and innovation ecosystems.

Rather than preparing learners for a single job, the framework develops adaptable professionals capable of growing throughout their careers as technologies evolve.

The framework emphasizes practical skills, real-world experience, continuous learning, and lifelong professional development.

---

# Vision

Enable every learner to develop the knowledge, skills, experience, and professional mindset required to thrive in the Intelligence Economy.

---

# Objectives

The Career Framework aims to:

- Develop industry-ready professionals
- Build practical engineering capabilities
- Support lifelong learning
- Encourage innovation and entrepreneurship
- Promote responsible leadership
- Prepare learners for future technologies
- Foster interdisciplinary collaboration

---
