# School of Artificial Intelligence

> *Building intelligent systems that learn, reason, adapt, and collaborate across classical and emerging computing paradigms.*

---

# Overview

The **School of Artificial Intelligence** provides a structured learning pathway covering the theory, engineering, and practical application of Artificial Intelligence across research, enterprise, scientific computing, and next-generation intelligent systems.

The curriculum combines classical AI, modern Machine Learning, Generative AI, Agentic AI, and Hybrid Quantum-AI (QAI) concepts to prepare learners for the future of intelligent engineering.

This school is part of the **FAEP Academy**, where education is integrated with research, innovation, professional development, and lifelong learning.

---

# Vision

Develop engineers, researchers, architects, and innovators capable of designing trustworthy, scalable, and intelligent systems for Industry 5.0, Society 5.0, Cloud 4.0, and future autonomous ecosystems.

Learners will be able to:
