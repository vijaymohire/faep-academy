# Quantum Information Science

Quantum Information Science (QIS) is the interdisciplinary field that combines quantum physics, information theory, computer science, mathematics, and engineering to understand how information can be represented, processed, transmitted, and protected using the principles of quantum mechanics.

Unlike classical information, quantum information exploits superposition, entanglement, interference, and quantum measurement to enable entirely new approaches to computation, communication, sensing, cryptography, and intelligent systems.

The FAEP Academy Quantum Information Science learning pathway provides a comprehensive engineering-oriented introduction to quantum information theory, quantum states, quantum particles, quantum measurement, quantum communication, and the scientific foundations that underpin Quantum Computing, Quantum AI (QAI), Quantum Error Correction, and future quantum technologies.

Rather than focusing solely on theoretical concepts, this repository emphasizes scientific understanding, engineering applications, system integration, verification and validation, and multidisciplinary research that prepares learners for the rapidly evolving quantum ecosystem.

This repository serves as the scientific foundation for advanced learning across the FAEP Academy quantum engineering curriculum.
