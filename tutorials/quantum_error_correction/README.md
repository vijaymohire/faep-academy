# Quantum Error Correction (QEC)

Quantum Error Correction (QEC) is one of the foundational engineering disciplines that enables reliable quantum computation. Unlike classical computers, quantum systems are highly susceptible to errors arising from decoherence, environmental interactions, imperfect quantum gates, measurement uncertainty, and operational noise. Quantum Error Correction provides the theoretical and engineering methodologies required to detect, diagnose, and correct these errors while preserving fragile quantum information.

The FAEP Academy Quantum Error Correction learning pathway provides a comprehensive engineering-oriented introduction to quantum noise, error models, quantum error correcting codes, logical qubits, syndrome extraction, fault-tolerant computation, and scalable quantum architectures.

Rather than treating QEC as a purely mathematical discipline, this repository emphasizes practical engineering, verification and validation, system reliability, hardware-aware error correction, hybrid classical-quantum decoding, and the transition toward Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing (FTQC).

The repository serves as the primary learning resource for understanding how reliable quantum computers are engineered and provides the foundation for future large-scale quantum computing, Quantum AI (QAI), and next-generation intelligent computing platforms.

By studying this repository, learners will be able to:
