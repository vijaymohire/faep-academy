# Quantum AI (QAI)

---

# Overview

The **Quantum AI (QAI)** repository serves as the central learning hub for Quantum Artificial Intelligence within the FAEP Academy. It provides a structured introduction to the concepts, architectures, engineering methodologies, platforms, and technologies that combine Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, Systems Engineering, and Applied Research into an integrated engineering ecosystem.

Rather than treating AI and Quantum Computing as independent disciplines, the QAI learning path explores how classical and quantum technologies collaborate to solve complex scientific, engineering, industrial, and societal challenges.

The repository provides educational resources covering the complete QAI lifecycle—from foundational principles and architectures to engineering frameworks, hybrid runtimes, intelligent platforms, digital twins, knowledge graphs, validation methodologies, and future Quantum AI systems.

QAI within the FAEP Academy emphasizes practical engineering, applied research, systems thinking, continuous innovation, verification and validation, and responsible technology development.

---

# Learning Objectives

After completing this learning path, learners should be able to:

- Understand the principles of Quantum Artificial Intelligence.
- Explain the relationship between classical AI and quantum computing.
- Explore hybrid classical-quantum architectures.
- Understand QAI engineering frameworks and methodologies.
- Study QAI platforms, runtimes, and cloud ecosystems.
- Design intelligent systems using systems engineering principles.
- Apply verification and validation techniques for QAI solutions.
- Explore digital twins, knowledge graphs, intelligent agents, and world models.
- Conduct applied research using the QAI engineering methodology.
- Develop future-ready Quantum AI solutions for industry, research, and society.

QAI combines Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, Systems Engineering, Cloud Computing, Digital Engineering, Applied Research, and Engineering Validation into a unified framework that supports the design, development, deployment, and continuous evolution of intelligent systems.

Rather than focusing solely on algorithms or quantum hardware, the FAEP Academy promotes a holistic engineering approach where learners understand the complete lifecycle of intelligent systems—from scientific foundations and architecture to engineering implementation, operational management, and long-term evolution.

- Systems thinking
- Multidisciplinary engineering

- Continuous innovation

Learners are encouraged to investigate not only how intelligent systems function, but also why they function, how they evolve, and how they can be engineered responsibly for real-world applications.

# Getting Started

Whether you are a student, researcher, engineer, startup founder, educator, or industry professional, the Quantum AI (QAI) learning pathway provides a structured introduction to the multidisciplinary engineering principles that combine Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, Systems Engineering, Digital Engineering, Cloud Computing, and Applied Research.

We recommend the following learning journey:

1. Begin with the QAI Foundations to understand the core concepts and engineering principles.
2. Explore the QAI Frameworks and Architecture repositories to understand system design and engineering methodologies.
3. Study the Platform, Runtime, Cloud, and Hybrid Runtime repositories to understand intelligent system execution.
4. Learn about Knowledge Graphs, Digital Twins, Enterprise Intelligence, and World Models to develop intelligent information systems.
5. Explore QAI Models, Agents, Workflows, and Ecosystems to understand intelligent automation.
6. Study Security, Validation, and Applied Research methodologies to develop reliable and trustworthy engineering solutions.
7. Apply your knowledge through laboratories, projects, and engineering experimentation.
8. Continue exploring Future QAI research topics while contributing to the broader FAEP Academy ecosystem.

The QAI learning pathway encourages multidisciplinary thinking, practical engineering, continuous experimentation, and lifelong learning.
