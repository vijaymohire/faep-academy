# Quantum Networking

Quantum Networking is an emerging engineering discipline focused on connecting quantum devices, processors, memories, sensors, and communication systems to enable secure, distributed, and scalable quantum information exchange. It forms the technological foundation for the Quantum Internet, distributed quantum computing, quantum cloud platforms, and future Quantum AI (QAI) ecosystems.

Unlike classical computer networks that exchange digital information, quantum networks transmit quantum states while preserving coherence, entanglement, and information integrity across geographically distributed systems. Achieving this requires specialized communication protocols, quantum repeaters, quantum memories, error management, synchronization, and intelligent network orchestration.

The FAEP Academy Quantum Networking learning pathway provides an engineering-oriented introduction to quantum communication protocols, network architectures, quantum repeaters, distributed quantum systems, quantum internet technologies, and hybrid classical-quantum networking.

Rather than focusing solely on communication protocols, this repository explores Quantum Networking as a complete engineering discipline spanning physics, communication engineering, distributed systems, systems engineering, runtime orchestration, cybersecurity, and intelligent quantum infrastructure.

This repository provides the engineering foundation for scalable distributed quantum platforms supporting Quantum Computing, Quantum Memory, Quantum Error Correction, Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing (FTQC), and future Quantum AI systems.
