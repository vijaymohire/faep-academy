# Quantum Sensing

Quantum Sensing is an emerging engineering discipline that leverages quantum mechanical phenomena to perform measurements with unprecedented precision, sensitivity, and accuracy. By exploiting quantum coherence, superposition, entanglement, and quantum interference, quantum sensors enable capabilities beyond the limits of many classical sensing technologies.

Quantum Sensing has applications across scientific research, healthcare, aerospace, navigation, telecommunications, manufacturing, environmental monitoring, defense, robotics, autonomous systems, and intelligent infrastructure. As quantum technologies mature, sensing is expected to become one of the earliest and most widely adopted commercial quantum technologies.

The FAEP Academy Quantum Sensing learning pathway provides an engineering-oriented introduction to quantum measurement principles, sensing technologies, instrumentation, distributed sensing networks, hybrid sensing systems, and intelligent data processing.

Rather than focusing solely on sensor physics, this repository explores Quantum Sensing as an integrated engineering discipline spanning quantum physics, instrumentation engineering, signal processing, systems engineering, artificial intelligence, and enterprise-scale sensing platforms.

This repository provides the engineering foundation for next-generation intelligent sensing systems supporting Quantum Computing, Quantum Networking, Quantum AI (QAI), autonomous systems, and future cyber-physical ecosystems.
