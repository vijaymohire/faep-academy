# Engineering Validation & Verification

Engineering Validation & Verification (V&V) is a systematic engineering discipline that ensures systems are designed correctly, built correctly, integrated correctly, and satisfy stakeholder needs throughout their complete lifecycle.

Modern engineering systems—including software, artificial intelligence, quantum computing, robotics, autonomous systems, aerospace platforms, semiconductor devices, digital twins, and large-scale enterprise systems—require rigorous validation methodologies to achieve reliability, safety, security, scalability, and operational readiness.

The FAEP Academy Engineering Validation & Verification learning pathway provides a multidisciplinary framework covering verification principles, validation methodologies, testing strategies, simulation, formal verification, model validation, quality engineering, systems assurance, technology readiness, operational evaluation, and continuous improvement.

Rather than treating testing as a final development activity, this repository presents validation as a continuous engineering capability that spans requirements, architecture, implementation, integration, deployment, operations, maintenance, and future system evolution.

This repository provides engineering foundations supporting trustworthy systems, resilient architectures, responsible AI, Quantum AI (QAI), safety-critical systems, and future intelligent engineering ecosystems.

# Learning Philosophy

The Engineering Validation & Verification learning pathway emphasizes that quality must be engineered into systems from the earliest stages of development rather than inspected only after implementation.

Validation is treated as a continuous engineering discipline that integrates requirements engineering, architecture, implementation, testing, operational evaluation, and continuous improvement throughout the complete system lifecycle.

The FAEP Academy learning philosophy is based upon:
