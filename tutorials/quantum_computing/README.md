# Quantum Computing

Quantum Computing is an emerging computing paradigm that leverages the principles of quantum mechanics to solve problems that are difficult or impractical for classical computers. By utilizing quantum phenomena such as superposition, entanglement, and interference, quantum computers offer new approaches to computation, optimization, simulation, cryptography, and scientific discovery.

The FAEP Academy Quantum Computing learning pathway provides a structured engineering-oriented introduction to quantum technologies, combining scientific foundations, quantum algorithms, quantum hardware, programming, error correction, hybrid computing, and future fault-tolerant quantum systems.

Rather than focusing solely on theory, this repository emphasizes practical engineering, system design, verification, validation, and the integration of quantum computing into real-world applications and multidisciplinary engineering ecosystems.

The repository serves as the primary entry point for understanding quantum computing within the broader FAEP Academy and provides the foundation for advanced learning in Quantum AI (QAI), hybrid computing, intelligent systems, and future computational technologies.

---

# Learning Objectives

By studying this repository, learners will be able to:

- Understand the principles of quantum mechanics relevant to computing

- Study quantum networking and distributed quantum systems
- Understand quantum error correction and fault tolerance
- Explore hybrid classical-quantum computing
- Apply verification and validation techniques for quantum systems

- Integrate quantum computing with Quantum AI and intelligent engineering platforms

# Hybrid Computing Framework

Modern quantum computing relies heavily on hybrid architectures that integrate classical and quantum resources.

The framework includes:

- Classical Computing
- Quantum Processing Units (QPUs)
- GPU Acceleration
- High-Performance Computing (HPC)
- Cloud Computing
- Distributed Systems
- Runtime Orchestration

Hybrid execution enables efficient utilization of quantum resources while leveraging mature classical computing infrastructures.
