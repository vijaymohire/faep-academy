# Space Systems

Space Systems Engineering is the multidisciplinary engineering discipline responsible for designing, developing, integrating, operating, and sustaining complex systems that enable exploration, observation, communication, scientific discovery, and human activities beyond Earth.

Modern space systems extend far beyond satellites and launch vehicles. They integrate aerospace engineering, systems engineering, artificial intelligence, robotics, digital twins, communications, cloud computing, cybersecurity, autonomous operations, and increasingly, advanced computing technologies to support scientific missions and future space infrastructure.

The FAEP Academy Space Systems learning pathway provides an engineering-oriented framework covering the complete lifecycle of space systems—from scientific foundations and mission planning through spacecraft design, satellite operations, autonomous systems, verification & validation, and future intelligent space ecosystems.

Rather than viewing spacecraft as isolated assets, this repository explores space systems as interconnected engineering ecosystems spanning Earth, orbital infrastructure, lunar exploration, planetary missions, and future distributed scientific environments.

This repository provides the engineering foundation for next-generation intelligent space platforms supporting scientific research, aerospace innovation, autonomous missions, and future federated space infrastructures.
