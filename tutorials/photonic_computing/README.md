# Photonic Computing

> Learn the principles, architectures, and engineering applications of light-based computing systems that enable ultra-fast, energy-efficient information processing for Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Technologies, and future intelligent computing platforms.

---

# Overview

Photonic Computing is an emerging computing paradigm that uses photons instead of electrons to process, transmit, and manipulate information. By leveraging the unique properties of light, photonic systems offer the potential for extremely high bandwidth, low latency, reduced power consumption, and massive parallelism.

Within the FAEP Academy, Photonic Computing is presented as an interdisciplinary engineering field that combines optics, physics, computer engineering, semiconductor technologies, Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, and integrated photonics to develop next-generation computing platforms.

This learning path introduces the scientific foundations, engineering principles, hardware architectures, software ecosystems, and future applications of photonic computing across research and industry.
