# Agriculture

## Purpose

This folder provides curated navigation for FAEP Academy learners
and industry stakeholders exploring the application of QAI, CPS,
Digital Twins, Edge AI and emerging quantum technologies to agriculture.

The Agriculture industry track is based on the NSW smart-agriculture
pilot and its post-pilot technology roadmap.

## Industry Architecture

The Agriculture solution is organized around three complementary paths:

- **Computational Path** — classical computing, AI, optimization,
  HPC and selective QAI/quantum execution.
- **Sensing Path** — IoT sensors, vision, MEMS, NEMS and future
  advanced/quantum sensing.
- **Communication Path** — farm/edge/cloud connectivity, QAI network
  overlays and future photonic/quantum communication research.

These paths operate across the CPS, Digital Thread, Digital Twin,
QAI and cloud/edge architecture.

## Pilot

The Agriculture Pilot demonstrates a practical pathway from:

**Sensors → Edge → Data → AI/ML → Optimization → QAI → Digital Twin
→ Decision Support → Measured Outcome**

The pilot is intended to establish measurable technical and operational
evidence before additional QAI capabilities are introduced.

## Post-Pilot

The post-pilot roadmap provides optional extensions including:

- Advanced Edge and COTS platforms
- QAI Edge Runtime and QAI Control Plane
- QAI-HAFL and QAI Runtime
- Digital Twin expansion
- Advanced sensing
- MEMS / NEMS / QEMS / Q-NEMS research
- Water intelligence and desalination optimization
- Greenhouse intelligence
- Climate resilience
- Nanotechnology-enabled sensing
- HPC and classical fallback
- QAI Hub and external connectivity
- Virtual Qubit Fabric
- Transduction Fabric
- QAI communication overlays
- Photonic and quantum communication research
- Communication-QEC research

QAI products and advanced quantum capabilities in the post-pilot
roadmap are under development or research unless explicitly identified
as available capabilities.
