# FAEP Academy Mathematics

> Mathematics-based navigation that organizes educational resources according to the theoretical foundations required for engineering, scientific computing, artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and multidisciplinary problem solving.

---

# Overview

The **math** directory provides a foundation-oriented view of the FAEP Academy.

Rather than organizing educational resources by technology, profession, industry, or engineering methodology, mathematics navigation groups notebooks, tutorials, laboratories, projects, and case studies according to the mathematical concepts that underpin them.

Each mathematical topic references reusable educational assets without duplicating content.

---

# Purpose

Mathematics navigation helps answer questions such as:

- What mathematics do I need before studying AI?
- Which notebooks require Linear Algebra?
- Where can I learn Probability and Statistics?
- Which mathematical foundations support Quantum Computing?
- What concepts are needed for Optimization?

Mathematics provides the theoretical foundation upon which engineering knowledge is built.

# FAEP Perspective

Mathematics is the universal foundation of engineering and scientific discovery.

Whether studying Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, Robotics, Systems Engineering, Digital Twins, or Enterprise Architecture, learners benefit from understanding the mathematical principles that support those disciplines. Mathematics-based navigation encourages learners to strengthen theoretical understanding while reusing the same educational assets across multiple domains, methodologies, and professional roles.

## FAEP Education

Building mathematically grounded learning experiences that connect reusable engineering knowledge assets with rigorous theoretical foundations, enabling learners to bridge mathematics, engineering, science, and real-world innovation.
